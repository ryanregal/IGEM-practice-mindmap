Quiz & Engagement

Test your understanding of the lessons and complete the quiz to check your preparedness level.

1

What is the first step when a flood warning is issued?

Correct!

During a flood warning, moving quickly to higher ground keeps you safe and gives rescue teams room to work.

2

Which item should be included in a flood emergency kit?

Correct!

A good flood emergency kit should include clean water, first aid, and basic supplies so you can stay safe if help is delayed.

3

What is the best attitude during flood preparation?

Answer

Correct: Stay calm and act proactively

A calm, prepared mindset helps you make safer choices and avoid mistakes when flood threats are near.
